Baking the Egg Tarts

  1. Take out a oven baking tray
  2. Put 6 egg tart shells into baking tray
  3. Pour the egg custard into the shells
  4. Put the baking tray into the oven
  5. Set the oven to 180°C for 40 minutes.
  6. Start the oven
  7. Wait for 40 minutes.
  8. Your egg tarts are ready! Let them cool before eating.
