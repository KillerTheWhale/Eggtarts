Making the Egg Custard

  1. Grab the bowl or container you would like to use for mixing
  2. Crack 3 whole eggs into the bowl
  3. Mix the 3 eggs evenly
  4. Add in 80g of condensed milk
  5. Mix everything up evenly
  6. Add in 125g and 125ml of milk
  7. Add in 250g or 250ml of milk
  8. Mix everything up evenly

You just finished making the Egg Custard Base!
