Baking the Egg Tarts

  1. Take out the basket of the air fryer.
  2. Put 6 egg tart shells into the basket.
  3. Pour the egg custard into the shells.
  4. Put the basket back into the air fryer.
  5. Set the air fryer to 180°C for 15 minutes.
  6. Start the air fryer.
  7. Wait for 15 minutes.
  8. Your egg tarts are ready! Let them cool before eating.
